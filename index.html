Dasox

Designing a handheld vacuum cleaner for cleaning car interiors — compact enough to live in a door pocket, capable enough to reach every seam.

(01.1) Design concept

Clean the mess
on the spot.

A compact, user-friendly handheld vacuum that can be stored in small car compartments, so messes are cleaned the moment they happen. Portability and ease of use let it slip into daily routines — a practical, efficient way to keep a car interior clean.

Fig. 01 — 42 silhouette studies
(01.2) Research problem

Tight spaces. No time.
Bulky, weak tools.

Cleaning car interiors is tough: current vacuums are uncomfortable, lack portability, or need constant emptying. A compact, powerful and ergonomic solution is needed.

Observed gaps in the market
  1. 01Lack of integrated tools
  2. 02Limited portability
  3. 03Poor ergonomics
Target group
ParentsLong-distance commutersBusiness professionalsFrequent travelers
(01.3) Car interior curves analysis

Field research found that dirt collects in tight, curved spaces — between seats, under pedals, along floor mats, around door handles and cupholders — pointing to a tool with precision attachments like a narrow crevice nozzle.

(01.4) Storyboard

Messy car → food spill → pistol grip → air-blow → tight spaces → storage → charging

(01.5) Name & inspiration
Dash

The sleek, compact design mirrors the dynamic nature of a car’s dashboard — and conveys speed and efficiency for quick, on-the-go cleaning.

+
Axis

The ergonomic, functional features — a rotatable handle and adaptable attachments — give versatile movement and precision in tight spaces.

Masar

A durable shoe and bag combo designed for low-income students who are always on the go.

  • TypeUniversity project, individual
  • Year2023
  • SupervisorsProf. Miroslaw Leczek, Assoc. Prof. Riccardo Falletta & TA Mennatallah Nabeel
(03.1) Problem & concept
0%

of Egypt’s population lives below the poverty line (CAPMAS, 2021)

Many underprivileged children struggle to find durable, affordable footwear and backpacks for school. Masar pairs durable, comfortable sneakers for difficult terrain with a spacious, practical backpack. A unisex design means it can be shared and passed down between siblings — a long-lasting, cost-effective companion for daily journeys.

Unisex5—25 yrsStudentsLow-income Egyptians

I kept the sneaker minimal, using only the essential pattern components — reducing material waste, production time and cost.

(03.2) Prototyping

Made by
hand.

Shoe design starts with selecting a last, taping it and sketching the pattern. The pattern is then cut, assembled and sewn using materials chosen for breathability and durability. I learned pattern drawing and sewing to bring both the sneakers and their packaging to life.
